2. We act in two different roles

This matters, because it decides who you should contact about your data.

As a controller — our own website and enquiries

When you visit casrose.co.uk or contact us directly, we decide what we collect and why. We are the controller and we handle your request from start to finish.

As a processor — services we run for client businesses

We operate messaging and administration services for other companies. In that work the client business is the controller — it decides what is collected, why, and for how long — and we act on its documented instructions under a written agreement meeting UK GDPR Article 28.

If you are a worker or subcontractor and your employer or contractor uses one of our services, your data-rights requests are for that business to decide. We can't delete their records on our own initiative. You can still send your request to us — see our data deletion page — and we will acknowledge it, pass it to the business and help them action it. Contacting them directly is usually faster.

3. What we collect as a controller

  • Enquiries. Your name, email address, phone number if you give one, and what you write to us. Lawful basis: legitimate interests — responding to a business enquiry. Kept for 24 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Server logs. IP address, browser user-agent and pages requested, held by our hosting provider for security and fault-finding, then rotated out.

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies, and loads no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers.

4. What flows through the services we operate

Acting as a processor for client businesses, the following may pass through or be stored in our systems: WhatsApp phone numbers, names and nicknames, message content, photographs and videos sent to the service, records of work carried out, day rates and pay calculations, and tax-scheme status such as Construction Industry Scheme verification outcomes.

Our services are designed to hold the minimum needed to do the job. Identity documents, bank details, National Insurance numbers and tax reference numbers are deliberately kept out of the messaging systems and are handled directly between the worker and the client business or its bookkeeper.

6. Transfers outside the UK

Some of the providers above process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, together with the provider's own safeguards.

7. How long we keep things

For data we hold as a controller, see section 3. For data we process for a client business, the client's retention schedule applies; when a contract ends we return or delete the data on their instruction, except anything we are required to keep by law.

8. How we protect it

Access is limited to named accounts protected by two-factor authentication. Data is encrypted in transit. Credentials and access tokens are held in managed secret stores — never in messages, spreadsheets or documents.

9. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how it is used, and to receive it in a portable form. Some of these rights have limits — for example, records that must be kept for tax purposes.

Email cas@casrose.co.uk. We will acknowledge within five working days and respond within one month. Where we act as a processor we will pass your request to the controller and support them in answering it.

If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
